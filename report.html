Simpson's paradox -- treatment wins every segment and loses the average

scenario: heterogeneity

Treatment lifts every segment by +10%, yet the pooled number reads -24.9% because the arms carry different device mixes. Post-stratifying on the population share recovers +9.2%. Never ship on a pooled average without checking the mix.

  design              300,000 users/arm, true effect +10% in EVERY segment
  the bug             control is 40% mobile, treatment is 70% mobile

    segment     control    treatment    lift        verdict
    mobile      3.067%     3.416%   +11.35%    WIN (p=6.8e-08)
    desktop    11.752%    12.757%    +8.55%    WIN (p=4.4e-14)

    POOLED     -24.89%  (p = 5.54e-208)   <-- treatment 'loses'
    STRATIFIED  +9.23%  <-- recovers the truth (+10%)

    Every segment improved. The pooled average fell. Both are arithmetic.
